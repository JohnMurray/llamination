# Llamas
These are the main character for the game (llamanation). This will be the starting
character for the game (the village character) and will be used in a variety of
non-specialized roles.

# Golden Retrievers
These are primarily support units to other character types. For example, a llama
gathering resources (wood, stone, etc) could be "upgraded" to have a companion
golden retriever that would help them carry additional resources.

# Corgis
Advanced military unit. They are a versitile unit type that can be equipped for
different environment (through various upgrade paths). Units are upgraded _after_
they are created. When upgrading, the corgi's will appear as an agility-course
animation (haha).

# Wiener Dogs (dachshunds)
Advanced military unit. Dachshunds are a burrowing unit. They are limited in their
attack capabilities, but their burrow distance can be upgraded, allowing for surprise
attacks. 

# Beavers
Engineering, non-combat unit. Counterpart to the Groundhog. Beavers are an
engineering type unit for building in or over the water. This could include
bridges, shipyards, naval units, etc.

# Groundhogs
Engineering, non-compat unit. Counterpart to the Beaver. Groundhogs are an
engineering type unit for specialized building that requires significant manipulation
of terrain. This could be the construction of a tunnel, stairs (in a cliff face),
certain defensive fortifications, etc.
