# Practical Context
Llamanation is an RTS game, played from the "god perspective". You are always looking down at the world
you are playing and manipulating characters, harvesting resources, and building your base. A realistic
limitation of this world is that there is limited value in zooming in to the level of a single character
as the player is constantly working with the whole picture. 

The game will have an isometric view, giving the feeling of 3D while remaining strictly 2D. As such,
players will be able to move the camera on the x-y plane and zoom in and out but will not be able to
rotate the camera or change the viewing angle.

# Inspiration
This game is inspired by the RTS games of the 90's and early 00's, namely Ages of Empires, Command &
Conquer, and the Dune RTS series.

# Levity
Where Llamanation differs from its inspiration is the effort to feel a bit whimsical and light-hearted.
This will impact every faucet of the game and the art style will be the primary driver of conveying
this feeling. Maps need to feel "happy" or "visually interesting". This may be achieved through brighter
or bolder color choices for in game elements or by choosing more visually engaging environments
(e.g. no deserts).
